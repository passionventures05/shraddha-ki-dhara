# Owner's guide: updating the website

Open **your-domain.com/admin/** and sign in with GitHub. Changes appear on the live site about **1 to 2 minutes** after you press **Publish**.

## Add or change a puja kit
1. **Puja Kits → New Puja Kit** (or click an existing kit).
2. Fill in: **name** (English, and Hindi if you like), **photo**, **category**, the **three prices** (Basic, Standard, Premium), **what's in the kit**, and a short **description**.
3. **Show on website**: switch OFF to hide a kit for now (for example, out of stock). It is not deleted.
4. **Position in the list**: smaller numbers appear first.
5. Press **Publish**.

**Photos:** a clear, bright photo, at least 800 px wide and under 800 KB. Until you add one, a golden diya picture is shown.

## Change your phone, UPI or advance
**Website Content → Site details.** Here you set the WhatsApp number (digits only, with country code, e.g. `919876543210`),
UPI ID, notice hours (how early customers must order), the advance percentage, and the pincodes you deliver to.
If the pincode list is empty, customers simply ask you on WhatsApp whether you deliver to them.

## Every day, automatically
- **Mantra of the day** rotates through your list of mantras.
- **Next festival** counts down to the next date in your festival list. Please check dates against your panchang, and add next year's festivals when you can.
- At **aarti hours** (early morning and dusk) the site glows a little warmer.

## Good to know
- If you type something the site can't use (such as a price of 0 or a wrong UPI ID), the update is stopped and the previous site stays live. The error message in the repository's Actions tab says what to fix.
- Customers who tick **temple bell** in the top bar hear a soft bell when they add a kit. It is off until they choose it.
- Orders arrive as a WhatsApp message with all details. Customers pay the advance by UPI and send you the screenshot.
